Fueling AI With Real-Time Data:
New Technologies and Strategies

AI isn't failing because of models. It's failing because data arrives too late, lacks context, or breaks under production demands.

Real-time data is becoming essential for AI. In fact, 84% of data leaders say streaming is essential for AI success, according to recent industry research.

This shift to real-time, continuous data isn't just about streaming. It's driving new decisions around databases, data platforms, pipelines, and data delivery. How data flows end-to-end is now critical to supporting AI, analytics, and real-time decision-making.

This roundtable focuses on what's actually changing across data and analytics systems and how organizations are making data usable the moment it's needed.

What You'll Learn

  • Where AI systems break when data is delayed or inconsistent
  • How to capture, process, and deliver data in real time at scale
  • What it takes to make data usable the moment it's created
  • Which decisions matter most for real-time workloads
